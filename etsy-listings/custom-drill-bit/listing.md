## Why these choices

- **Title** leads with "Custom 3D Printed PDC Drill Bit Replica" because Etsy weights the first 40 characters most heavily. "Any Team or Company Logo" and "Oilfield Gift" hit the two buyer groups: fans and oil-and-gas.
- **Tags** avoid repeating words that are already in the title except where they form a distinct phrase buyers search ("oilfield gift", "roughneck gift").
- **Price at $120 with free personalization** avoids Etsy's "Prices vary" badge, which lowers click-through.
- **Team names are not in the tags.** Etsy's search can flag trademarked names on tags even when you hold a license, which can get a listing deactivated. Team names belong in the personalization box and your messages instead.
