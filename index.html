Join the Team

What we look for

  • Consistency over raw pace — you can string clean laps together for a full stint.
  • A safety rating you're proud of. Clean racecraft matters more than your iRating.
  • Reliable comms: you show up for practice, you're on Discord, you call what you see.
  • Endurance mindset — fuel saving, tire management, and patience in traffic.
  • Good teammate energy. We spend long nights in voice chat together.

How to apply

  1. Join our Discord and say hi in the intros channel.
  2. Tell us your iRating, safety rating, usual series, and the cars you love.
  3. Run a practice session with us — one relaxed evening stint, no pressure.
  4. If it clicks on both sides, you're on the grid for the next event.
